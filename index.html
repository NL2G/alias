01 / The project+

About ALIAS

ALIAS studies how LLMs can tell whether two German texts were written by the same author, together with a German law enforcement agency.

02 / Research pillars+

Six pillars

Six questions for authorship verification in practice.

Pillar 01

German benchmarks

Most authorship research is in English. We build large German benchmarks across platforms, genres and time.

Pillar 02

Checkable evidence

A score alone is not enough. We show which style features support a decision, so investigators can check it.

Pillar 03

Worth the cost?

LLMs are the most accurate, but costly. We measure when their gains justify the compute.

Pillar 04

Shareable data

Case data is confidential. We study when synthetic German text can replace it for training and testing.

Pillar 05

When do LLMs win?

We compare LLM-based methods with traditional authorship methods on the same benchmarks, to see where each one wins.

Pillar 06

Beyond the training data

Real cases rarely look like the training data. We test how verification holds up when the genre, topic or time period changes.
